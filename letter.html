FOR YOU

Dear Amurwabhumi,

I don't really know where to start, so I'll just say it.

Thank you for existing in my life. Seriously. Out of all the random people in this world, somehow i got to meet you. And that's something i don't think I'll ever take for granted.

I know we won't always have perfect days. There will be misunderstandings, bad moods, busy days, and moments when things don't go exactly the way we want them to.

But even knowing that, I'd still choose to know you. I'd still choose all the little conversations, the random moments, the laughs, and even the things we don't always agree on.

Because somewhere along the way, you became someone who matters to me more than i ever expected.

And honestly, that's kinda crazy. I wasn't looking at the beginning of our story thinking, “yeah, this person is going to become this important to me.”

It just happened. Slowly. Naturally. And before i knew it, there was you.

I hope you know that you are appreciated. Not just for the big things, but for the tiny things too. The things you probably don't even realize I notice.

So if someday you ever wonder whether meeting you meant something to me, the answer is simple.

It did. It does. And i hope it keeps meaning something for a really, really long time.

Happy boyfriend day, i'm glad our paths crossed.
9 August 2026.

— yours, kirana.
